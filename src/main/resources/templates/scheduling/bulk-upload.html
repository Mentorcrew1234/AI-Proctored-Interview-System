Schedule interviews in bulk

Create a whole drive at once from a spreadsheet. Every row goes through the same validation as the single form, so a bulk-scheduled interview is an ordinary interview.

1 Download template 2 Fill it in 3 Upload & check 4 Confirm

1 · Download the template

Download .xlsx

One row per interview, plus a second sheet listing the accepted values for every column. It is generated fresh each time, so the example dates are always valid.

2 · Fill it in

Do not add interview questions — they are generated by the AI when the candidate starts. If a candidate account does not exist yet it is created, and its password is shown once on the results page.

Column reference
Column Required Accepted values
Interview NameYesYour name for the drive, e.g. "Java Developer - Campus Drive 2026". Many rows may share one.
Candidate NameYesAny text
Candidate EmailYesA valid email address
Interview DateYesYYYY-MM-DD, not in the past
Interview TimeYes24-hour HH:MM
DomainYesJava, Python, Web Development, Database, Software Engineering, Data Structures & Algorithms
Experience TypeYesFRESHER or EXPERIENCED
Years of ExperienceIf EXPERIENCED1–50. Leave blank for a fresher.
LanguageYesEnglish
Interview TypeYesTECHNICAL or HR_GENERAL
Question CountNo1–10, defaults to 5
College NameNoUp to 150 characters. Only used when the candidate account has to be created; an existing account is never altered by an upload.
LocationNoUp to 120 characters. Creation only, same as College Name.
SkillsNoComma-separated, up to 255 characters. Each becomes a filter option on the candidates page. Creation only.

3 · Upload for checking

Uploading only checks the file. Nothing is scheduled until you review the results and confirm on the next screen.
